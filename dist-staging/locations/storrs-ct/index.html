About this Peg

Italian food & wood-fired pizza in Storrs

Our founders are UConn alumni, so Storrs Center feels like coming home. Dog Lane is where Husky fans land after the game and alumni reunions come together, and the oven runs until 1am on Fridays and Saturdays.

Every pie starts with dough made fresh daily and never frozen. Choose a red or white Neo-Neapolitan round or a crispy-edged Detroit-style pie, build your own, or go gluten-free with our 12″ crust. Vegan cheese is available on any pizza.

Not in a pizza mood? The kitchen turns out Italian-American comfort food too: chicken parmesan, pasta alla vodka, pasta bolognese, shrimp scampi, spaghetti & meatballs, eggplant parm, chicken and meatball parm sandwiches, salads, wood-fired wings and a kids’ menu. Pair it with a cocktail, a glass of wine or a cold beer.

Good to know

Storrs FAQ

What are Square Peg Storrs’s hours?

Mon–Tue 11:30am–9pm; Wed 11:30am–10pm; Thu 11:30am–12am; Fri 11:30am–1am; Sat 11am–1am; Sun 11am–10pm. Holiday hours may vary; online ordering always shows live availability.

Is Square Peg Storrs an Italian restaurant?

Yes. Along with wood-fired pizza, Square Peg Storrs serves Italian-American favorites like chicken parmesan, pasta alla vodka, pasta bolognese and shrimp scampi, plus chicken and meatball parm sandwiches, salads, wood-fired wings and desserts.

Is Square Peg Storrs a good restaurant for families?

Yes. There’s a kids’ menu (pasta, spaghetti and meatballs, chicken fingers, mac and cheese), room for big groups, and large party reservations for birthdays and team dinners.

Does Square Peg Storrs serve beer, wine and cocktails?

Yes. Square Peg Storrs serves beer, wine and cocktails with dine-in meals, so it works for date nights, game nights and dinner with friends.

Can I order online from Square Peg Storrs?

Yes. Tap “Order Storrs online” to order for pickup, or choose delivery at checkout where it’s available.

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

Yes. We offer a 12″ gluten-free crust, and vegan cheese can be added to any pizza.

Does Square Peg Storrs do catering?

Yes. Storrs caters birthdays, office lunches, team events and more. Send a quick request on our catering page, or call (860) 454-6038.

Is Square Peg Storrs close to Mansfield and Coventry?

Yes. Square Peg Storrs at 9 Dog Ln in Storrs is a short drive from Mansfield, Coventry, Willington, Ashford and Tolland. Order ahead online for pickup, or check delivery availability at checkout.

Can our group host a fundraiser here?

Yes. Every Tuesday from 4pm to close, one organization earns 20% of dine-in food sales from its supporters.
