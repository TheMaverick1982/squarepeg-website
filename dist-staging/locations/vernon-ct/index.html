About this Peg

Wood-fired pizza in Vernon

Right on Talcottville Road (Route 83), Vernon is the easy stop for Rockville, Ellington and Tolland: pickup on the way home, or a late one on Friday when the oven runs until midnight.

Every pie starts with dough made fresh daily and never frozen. Choose a red or white signature pie, build your own, or go gluten-free with our 12″ crust. Vegan cheese is available on any pizza.

Vernon FAQ

What are Square Peg Vernon’s hours?

Mon–Tue 11:30am–9pm; Wed–Thu 11:30am–10pm; Fri 11:30am–12am; Sat 11am–11pm; Sun 11am–9pm. Holiday hours may vary; online ordering always shows live availability.

Can I order online from Square Peg Vernon?

Yes. Tap “Order Vernon online” to order for pickup, or choose delivery at checkout where it’s available.

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

Yes. We offer a 12″ gluten-free crust, and vegan cheese can be added to any pizza.

Does Square Peg Vernon do catering?

Yes. Vernon caters birthdays, office lunches, team events and more. Send a quick request on our catering page, or call (860) 926-0088.

Is Square Peg Vernon close to Rockville and Ellington?

Yes. Square Peg Vernon at 226 Talcottville Rd in Vernon is a short drive from Rockville, Ellington, Tolland, Manchester and South Windsor. Order ahead online for pickup, or check delivery availability at checkout.

Can our group host a fundraiser here?

Yes. Every Tuesday from 4pm to close, one organization earns 20% of dine-in food sales from its supporters.
