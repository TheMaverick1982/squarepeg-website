How we make it

Water. Flour. Time. Fire.

Every dough ball starts in one of our two commissary kitchens, East Hartford for Connecticut and Delray Beach for Florida, and gets stretched by hand before it hits the wood-fired oven. We roast, simmer and season with purpose, and it’s worth it for the head-tilt, the smile and the “wow” after the first bite.
